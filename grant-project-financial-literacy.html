Project overview

Knowledge for everyday financial decisions

The Financial Times Financial Literacy and Inclusion Campaign provides free and engaging financial education intended to support more confident choices and greater social mobility.

The initiative focuses on young people, women and underserved groups, including minority ethnic communities and migrants.

Foundation Trustee Anabel Altenburg was a founding member and helped design and deliver learning modules.

Who the project serves

Financial learning made more accessible

Financial knowledge can influence day-to-day decisions, resilience and the ability to pursue longer-term opportunities.

The initiative was developed to make that learning approachable for people who may not otherwise have easy access to it.
