About the Fellow

Meet Audi Ahmed

Audi is a founder, operator and Chevening Scholar completing an MSc in Innovation, Entrepreneurship and Strategy at SOAS University of London.

After studying Civil Engineering at the Sudan University of Science and Technology, Audi built a career across entrepreneurship, venture support, startup training and business development.

Professional & academic background

Experience shaped by service

In 2020, Audi founded Tagga for Food Services, initially connecting local home chefs with customers before developing the organisation's business-to-business catering work.

After war began in Sudan, Audi relocated to Cairo and co-founded 117 Business Talk to support displaced Sudanese entrepreneurs rebuilding their networks and businesses.

Audi has also worked with startup and social-enterprise founders through acceleration, mentoring and consultancy roles, and is now expanding expertise in strategy, finance and investment in London.

Impact journey

Founder capacity and economic inclusion

Practical training, venture support and community-building that connect Sudanese entrepreneurs with knowledge, networks and opportunity.

Audi's impact is best understood as a connected entrepreneurial and community-building journey rather than a single initiative: growing Tagga, the organisation Audi founded, mentoring founders, supporting displaced entrepreneurs and building the Sudanese Business Community.

This work focuses on strengthening founders' practical capabilities, helping them build stronger professional networks and improving inclusive access to economic opportunity.

Practical founder skills

Training and mentoring focus on the operational knowledge founders need to move forward.

Community after displacement

Support helps displaced entrepreneurs reconnect with peers, networks and opportunities.

Inclusive opportunity

The wider journey aims to make entrepreneurial knowledge and economic networks more accessible.
