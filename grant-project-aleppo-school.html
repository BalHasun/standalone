Project overview

A school restored for local learning

Through its partner IPV, the Altenburg Foundation supported a school in Daret Ezza in rural Aleppo for five years.

Support covered running costs, teacher and staff salaries, fuel, stationery and classroom rehabilitation for a school serving approximately 900 boys and girls.

The school later transitioned to local-authority operation, with continuing support provided by another non-governmental organisation.

Who the project serves

Education for children in rural Aleppo

The school served boys and girls in and around Daret Ezza, providing a local place for learning during a period of profound disruption.

The foundation's role focused on the practical costs and repairs needed to keep education operating before the later transition.
