About the Fellow

Meet Wisal Babiker

Wisal is a Sudanese medical doctor pursuing a joint MSc in Global Mental Health at King's College London and the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.

Clinical training and direct experience of conflict and social adversity shaped Wisal's commitment to accessible, culturally sensitive mental-health services for vulnerable communities.

Professional & academic background

Experience shaped by service

Wisal has worked with humanitarian organisations across Sudan, Syria and Egypt, including Médecins Sans Frontières, the Sudan Red Crescent and GOAL.

These roles included health-facility and community-based initiatives in complex and resource-limited settings, alongside continued volunteer service.

Postgraduate study is strengthening Wisal's expertise in psychosocial support, health equity and the research, policy and practice needed for responsive mental-health systems.

Community initiative

Lasta Wahdak

A community-based psychiatric rehabilitation organisation focused on reintegration, stigma reduction, public awareness and professional capacity-building in Sudan.

Lasta Wahdak is a community-based psychiatric rehabilitation organisation in Khartoum. Its work focuses on patient reintegration into communities, alongside public awareness and stigma reduction around mental ill health.

Conflict severely disrupted and suspended the organisation's activity. Relaunching operations and expanding its work are future-facing priorities rather than completed outcomes.

Community reintegration

Rehabilitation is centred on participation, dignity and reconnecting patients with community life.

Stigma reduction

Public awareness activity addresses barriers surrounding mental ill health.

Future relaunch

Rebuilding a sustainable pathway to relaunch and expand the organisation remains the next phase.
