Project overview

A route back into professional life

The Foundation has worked with RefuAid since 2020 to provide interest-free loans of up to £20,000 for people rebuilding professional pathways in the United Kingdom.

Loans can support UK accreditation or mandated professional training and, in relevant cases, day-to-day expenses while that process is under way.

As loans are repaid, the funds can support future recipients. The Foundation has also provided some support to people from underprivileged UK backgrounds and maintains professional connections and moral support where appropriate.

Who the project serves

Professional experience facing a practical barrier

Qualified people can face substantial costs before their existing skills and experience are recognised in the United Kingdom.

Interest-free support is intended to address that practical barrier without adding commercial interest charges to the route back into work.

Partnership in practice

What the partnership enables

01

Accreditation

Loans can contribute to the cost of UK professional accreditation.

02

Required training

Support can cover mandated training needed for a return to professional practice.

03

Day-to-day stability

Relevant cases may include help with living expenses during accreditation or training.

04

Revolving support

Repayments return to the fund so that the same capital can support future recipients.
