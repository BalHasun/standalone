Proposed initiative

HealSpace AI

A conceptual healthcare platform exploring personalised, calming digital experiences for children and young people during medical treatment.

HealSpace AI is a proposed platform for personalised emotional support during medical treatment for children and young people. The concept combines AI personalisation with calming multimedia, using mobile or tablet delivery.

Immersive or VR experiences could be used where resources allow. The concept remains in the early stages of development and does not claim deployment or clinical outcomes.

Personalised support

AI personalisation is being explored as a way to tailor calming content to individual needs.

Accessible delivery

The proposed experience is designed around mobile and tablet access.

Resource-aware immersion

Immersive or VR experiences remain optional and would be considered only where resources allow.
